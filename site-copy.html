01 / THE STORY

Why there is a PS5 room above the bakery

  • I did not plan to run a gaming lounge. I planned to stop hosting one in my flat. For about 4 years my living room in Kalyani Nagar was where 6 people turned up with 2 controllers, and someone was always on the floor at an angle where the goalkeeper was a smudge.
  • The nearest place that rented screens by the hour had PCs, a queue and nobody who could tell you whether the game you wanted was installed. So I took the first-floor shop I was already walking past every week — Above Kayani Bakery, opposite the HP pump — knocked out one wall, and opened with 3 bays, a whiteboard for bookings and no website at all.
  • 2 years on there are 5 bays and 10 seats. Almost everything that changed came from someone complaining at the counter. The folding chairs were miserable after an hour, so the recliners went in. Headsets are no fun when you came with a friend, so the corner bay got a sound bar. A fighting-game regular sat me down and showed me the difference on his own console, so the recliner and the paired bays got 120Hz panels.
  • I still take most of the bookings myself in the evenings. That is why the number on this site opens a WhatsApp chat with a person rather than a form that emails someone on Monday.
